Simulated reply

Plays a quiet 8-second test voice through the speaker. The orb reacts to its real output level. If the microphone is on, speaking over the test voice interrupts it and switches the orb to Listening.

Test voice: idle

Controls

Stop all turns off the microphone and the test voice and returns to Idle. Reset demo does the same and also clears the results. Pause freezes and dims the orb. The orb also pauses when the page is hidden.
